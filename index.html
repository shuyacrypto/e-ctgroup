Two products, each doing one job properly.

e-care247 holds the care record and the compliance picture. e-learn247 holds the training record and the certificates. They share a parent, an identity and a standard of work.

One system for the whole service. Evidence ready before it is asked for.

Care management for supported living and social care. Care plans, care notes, medicines, incidents and compliance in one system, across every registered location a provider runs.

Visit e-care247.co.uk
  • Care plans and risk assessments, with every version kept
  • Care notes for personal care, food and fluids, mood and health observations
  • eMAR with PRN limits, controlled drug witnessing and stock
  • Incidents and safeguarding, with a 5×5 risk matrix and Duty of Candour prompts
  • DoLS and restrictive practice records
  • DBS, right to work, training and property certificates in one view
  • A commissioner portal, with access granted and scoped by the provider
  • Evidence organised against the Single Assessment Framework quality statements

Mandatory care training in eight-minute modules.

Most carers do mandatory training on their day off. e-learn247 shortens modules so they can be finished on a break, ends each one in a real assessment, and keeps the training matrix live.

Visit e-learn247.co.uk
  • Mandatory care training in eight-minute modules
  • An assessment at the end of every module, with a pass mark and retry
  • Certificates mapped to the Single Assessment Framework quality statements
  • A matrix that opens from a share link, with no login

Your records stay yours.

Each organisation's data is separated by row-level security in the database itself. One provider's records cannot be read by another, whatever the application asks for.

Legal roles

The provider is the data controller. E-CT Group processes care data on the provider's instructions, under a written data processing agreement.

Hosting

e-care247 is hosted in Amsterdam, in the European Economic Area, which UK data protection law recognises as adequate.

Access

Role-based permissions. Support workers see the people they are rostered to support. Family and commissioner access is granted and scoped by the provider.

Record history

Care plan and risk assessment versions are kept, never overwritten, so a reviewer can see what a plan said on any given date.

Questions we are asked in every tender.

Short, direct answers. Where the answer is not yet, we say not yet.

Who is the data controller?

The provider. E-CT Group acts as data processor, on the provider's written instructions, under a data processing agreement.

Where is care data hosted?

e-care247 is hosted in Amsterdam, in the European Economic Area. UK data protection law recognises the EEA as adequate, so no extra transfer safeguards are needed.

Are you an NHS England assured Digital Social Care Records supplier?

Not yet. When we are, the listing and its reference will be shown on this page.

Do your products connect to NHS systems?

Not yet. Neither product currently exchanges data with NHS systems such as GP Connect. We will update this answer when that changes.

Can commissioners see the care they fund?

Yes, with the provider's agreement. The commissioner portal in e-care247 gives access to the records of the people you fund, and e-learn247's training matrix opens from a share link with no login.
